THE ARTISAN PATHWAY

Two Decades of Modern Habesha Elegance

Our founder, Eyerus (affectionately known as Jerry), started her journey in her early twenties. Captivated by the elegant rhythms of weaving on physical hand-looms, she recognized that traditional garments deserved to be enjoyed in daily life.

For twenty years, Jerry Design has used 100% pure organic cotton to bridge beautiful cultural expressions with active modern lives, transforming high-contrast textures into comfortable everyday fits.

We loom using 100% organic cotton fibers grown directly in Ethiopia. The signature color threads woven within symbolize Ethiopian history: rich Crimson, Gold, and Sky Blue. Lightweight, breathable, and designed to soften with every wear.

For our 100% cotton garments containing heavy handwoven golden stitching (Tilet), we suggest dry cleaning or gentle hand washing in cool water. Always line-dry out of direct intense midday sun to maintain rich colors.

Important First-Time Wash Instruction: For the first time washing, if the clothing is colored, it must be washed completely alone to prevent color transfer. Please avoid using powerful washing chemicals, bleach, or harsh synthetic detergents, as strong chemical agents might permanently damage or strip the natural vibrant colors of your handwoven fabric. Always use mild soap in cold or lukewarm water.
OUR FOUNDATION

Two Decades of Craft:
The Eyerus Story

"Every loom captures a distinct signature of cultural liberation."

Eyerus (popularly recognized in fashion spaces as Jerry) started carving her footprint into tailoring as a motivated artisan in her early twenties. Ethiopia was thriving with magnificent heritage styles, yet they were almost exclusively reserved for holiday ceremonies.

The Early Twenties Genesis

Armed with raw passion and traditional weaving lessons from rural masters, Eyerus began her experiments. She adapted the rigid structure of historic textiles to produce softer, lightweight everyday tunics made of 100% cotton for friends and colleagues, setting the core philosophy of Jerry Design.

A Brand Redefined

Establishing her first physical store, Jerry Design gained momentum for championing 100% traditional cotton materials styled with a contemporary Western fit. It was no longer clothing for special holidays only; it became clothing that defined everyday pride and comfort.

Preserving the Weaving Trade

Today, Jerry Design proudly employs localized artisan weavers, safeguarding traditional loom techniques that would have been replaced by industrial factories. Every single thread panel of our clothes is loomed using traditional physical rhythms with 100% pure organic cotton yarns.
